Purpose and operator

Sourav Sarkar operates this personal backup tool to copy files from his computer to his own Google Drive using rclone.

Google account access

The tool requests Google Drive access to locate backup folders, compare file metadata, and upload or update backup files. The configured backup uses copy operations and does not delete remote-only files. The Google authorization scope may permit broader Drive operations than this backup job uses.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in rclone's configuration. They are not published on this website or in its repository. This app does not sell data or intentionally share Drive files with third parties other than Google as the storage provider.

Control and retention

The owner can stop the scheduled job, revoke access in Google Account settings, and delete local or Drive backups. Copies remain until the owner deletes them. This static website does not provide access to the backups.
